# Osona Climbing Gym — member guide

## Opening hours

The gym opens Monday to Friday from 07:00 to 23:00, and weekends from 09:00 to
21:00. On public holidays it follows the weekend timetable. The bouldering room
closes 30 minutes before the gym.

## Prices and memberships

A single entry costs 12 euros. The monthly membership costs 45 euros and
includes unlimited access, one free guest pass per month, and a 10 percent
discount in the cafe. Students and under-18s pay 35 euros per month.

## Courses

The beginner course runs every first Monday of the month, lasts four sessions
and costs 80 euros including gear rental. The lead-climbing course requires
passing a belay test and costs 120 euros.

## Rules

Chalk must be used in a chalk ball inside the bouldering room. Brushing holds
is encouraged. Down-climbing is preferred over jumping. The gym provides free
liquid chalk at the entrance on humid days.
