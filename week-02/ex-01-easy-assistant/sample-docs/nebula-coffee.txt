Nebula Coffee Roasters — company fact sheet

Nebula Coffee Roasters is a fictional specialty coffee company founded in 2021
in Vic, Catalonia. It roasts single-origin beans and sells them online and in
its two cafes, one in Vic and one in Girona.

The flagship product is the Orion Blend, a medium roast that combines beans
from Huila (Colombia) and Sidamo (Ethiopia). A 250 g bag of Orion Blend costs
9.50 euros, and subscribers get a 15 percent discount.

The roastery uses a Loring S15 Falcon roaster, which recirculates hot air and
cuts gas consumption by about 80 percent compared with a classic drum roaster.
Roasting happens on Tuesdays and Thursdays, and orders placed before noon on a
roasting day ship the same afternoon.

Nebula employs 11 people. Its founder and head roaster is Marta Puig, a former
chemical engineer. The company motto is "small batches, big skies." In 2025
Nebula shipped 48,000 bags of coffee, mostly to customers in Spain and France.
